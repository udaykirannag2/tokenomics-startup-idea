# Interview Guide — AI Cost and Value

**Goal:** confirm or rule out the challenges in [challenges/ai-tokenomics-challenges.md](../challenges/ai-tokenomics-challenges.md). Learn what people do today, what it costs them, and what they'd pay to fix. Don't pitch a solution.

## Who to talk to

- FinOps leads and practitioners
- CFO, VP Finance, FP&A at SaaS companies
- Engineering leaders and platform/AI engineers
- SaaS product and pricing leads
- Procurement / IT asset management

Aim for 10–15 interviews across at least three of these roles.

## Questions (30 minutes)

**Context (5 min)**
1. What's your role, and how does AI spend touch your work?
2. Roughly how much does your org spend on AI each month, and on what (APIs, seats, GPUs, AI features in SaaS tools)?

**Spend and visibility (5 min)**
3. Walk me through the last time someone asked, "What are we spending on AI?" How did you answer, and how long did it take?
4. What AI spend can't you see or explain today?

**Value (10 min)**
5. Pick one AI use case. How do you know whether it's worth the money?
6. Who owns that answer? Engineering, finance or the business?
7. What happened the last time an AI project was cut or scaled? What data drove the decision?
8. If you could see one number for every AI use case, what would it be?

**Control and waste (5 min)**
9. Tell me about a surprise AI bill or overrun. What caused it?
10. Where do you suspect waste is, and why haven't you fixed it?

**Buying and pricing (3 min)**
11. How do you decide on AI contracts, commitments or plans?
12. (SaaS vendors) How do you price AI features, and do any customers cost you more than they pay?

**Close (2 min)**
13. What tools or spreadsheets do you use today, and what do you pay for them?
14. If this were solved, what would it be worth to you? Who else should I talk to?
